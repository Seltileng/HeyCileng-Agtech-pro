Waste for one business is raw material for another.

Every harvest leaves behind husks, stalks, peels and shells. Much of it is burned or dumped, while feed mills, fertilizer makers and bio-energy companies are looking for exactly these materials.

HeyCileng brings both sides together in one marketplace: sellers list what they have, and buyers find it by type, location and price, then deal with the seller directly.

How it works

  1. List

    Sellers list their waste with quantity, price per kg, location and photos.

  2. Match

    Buyers search by type, location and price, and compare what is on offer.

  3. Order Coming soon

    For now, buyers call the seller to agree price and collection. Online ordering with Paystack is on its way.

  4. Collect

    Collect or arrange delivery, and the waste goes on to its next use.
