HEYCILENG AGTECH - PLATFORM FEATURES
====================================
Every feature the website has today, taken from the actual code.
Section 15 lists what the platform does NOT do yet.


1. WHAT THE PLATFORM IS
-----------------------
An online marketplace for agricultural waste and byproducts.
- Sellers: farmers and agro-processors with crop waste to sell
  (cassava peels, rice husks, maize stalks, bagasse, shells and so on).
- Buyers: feed mills, fertilizer companies and bio-energy companies that
  use that waste as raw material.
Sellers list what they have. Buyers search, view details and phone the
seller directly. There is no online payment or ordering.


2. TECHNOLOGY
-------------
- Backend: Python with Flask 3.1 (one file: app.py).
- Database: SQLite (database/database.db), built into Python.
- Pages: Jinja2 HTML templates (templates/ folder).
- Styling: plain CSS (static/css/style.css for every page,
  static/css/home.css for the homepage only).
- Scripts: one plain JavaScript file (static/js/script.js), no frameworks.
- Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque (headings) and Public Sans (body text) from
  Google Fonts, with system fonts as a fallback.
- Run it: .\venv\Scripts\Activate.ps1, then python app.py,
  then open http://127.0.0.1:5000


3. PAGES AND ADDRESSES
----------------------
Anyone can open:
  /                          Landing page (homepage)
  /login                     Log in
  /register                  Sign up, step 1: choose Buyer or Seller
  /register/buyer            Sign up, step 2: buyer form
  /register/seller           Sign up, step 2: seller form
  /forgot-password           Request a password reset link
  /reset-password/<code>     Choose a new password (from the reset link)

Logged-in users only:

  /listings/<id>             Full details of one listing
  /seller/dashboard          Seller dashboard (sellers only)
  /buyer/dashboard           Buyer dashboard (buyers only)

Sellers only, and only for their own listings:
  /listings/new              Add a listing (the form is on the seller dashboard)
  /listings/<id>/edit        Edit a listing and its photos/video
  /listings/<id>/delete      Delete a listing

Old links such as /register?type=seller still work and go to the right form.
Unknown pages show a friendly "Page not found" page. Forbidden actions
show a friendly "Not allowed" page.


4. LANDING PAGE (/)
-------------------
Sections, top to bottom:
- Transparent header over the hero: HeyCileng logo, Home, About,
  Features, Contact, Login and an ochre "Sign Up" button (or Dashboard and
  Log out when logged in), plus the light/dark toggle.
- Hero: full-height rice-harvest photo with a dark green gradient, the
  headline "Farm remnants and spoils, back to work.", a short paragraph,
  and "Buy biomass" and "Sell your biomass" buttons.
- Stats band: 30-40% of harvests lost after the field; 15 million t of wet
  cassava peels produced in Nigeria each year; most of those peels dumped
  or burned. Sources (ThisDay, ILRI) are linked under the numbers.
- About: "Waste for one business is raw material for another." with a
  photo of farmers carrying their harvest.
- What you can buy: four photo cards (cassava peels, rice husks, sugarcane
  bagasse, crop stalks), each with a "See listings" button that opens a
  marketplace search.
- How it works: four numbered steps (List, Match, Order, Collect). Order is
  marked "Coming soon": buyers call the seller for now.
- Sell your biomass: three cards (farmers and cooperatives, agro-processors,
  aggregators and hubs), a "Start selling" button and "Already selling?
  Log in".
- Contact: a message form (name, email, message).
- Footer: Marketplace, How it works, About, Contact and Terms links,
  copyright and photo credits.


5. ACCOUNTS AND SIGN-UP
-----------------------
Two account types: Buyer and Seller. The type cannot be changed later.

Step 1, /register: "I want to" with two cards, Buy (feed mills,
fertilizer and energy companies) and Sell (farmers, cooperatives,
processors, aggregators).

Step 2, the form for the chosen role. The same two cards stay at the top
with the chosen one highlighted, so the role can be switched. The role
comes from the web address, so it stays the same through errors and cannot
be faked by editing the form.

Buyer form fields:
- Full name (required, up to 100 characters)
- Email address (required, must be valid, must not already be registered;
  capital letters are ignored)
- Phone number (required, 7-15 digits; spaces, dashes, brackets and a
  leading + are allowed)
- Password (required, 8-128 characters) with a Show/Hide button
- Confirm password (must match) with a Show/Hide button
- Company name (optional)
- Terms and Conditions checkbox (required)
- Button: "Create Account"

Seller form: the same fields, plus
- Business name (required, up to 100 characters)
- Business description (required, up to 1000 characters)
- Button: "Create Seller Account"

Validation:
- Every field is checked on the server. The browser also checks some
  fields first, including that the two passwords match.
- Each problem is shown in red under its field, and a light-red summary
  box appears above the form.
- What was typed is kept after an error, except passwords.
- After signing up, the user is logged in and taken to their dashboard
  with a welcome message.


6. LOGIN, LOGOUT AND PASSWORDS
------------------------------
Login (/login):
- Email and password, with a Show/Hide button on the password.
- "Remember me for 30 days" checkbox. Without it, the login ends when the
  browser is closed.
- "Forgot password?" link and a "Sign up" link.
- A wrong email or wrong password gives the same message, so nobody can
  find out which emails have accounts.
- Logged-in users who open login or sign-up pages go to their dashboard.

Logout:
- "Log out" at the bottom of the sidebar (or in the top navigation on
  public pages). It clears the session and shows "You have been logged out."

Forgot and reset password:
- Enter an email. The same reply is shown whether or not the account
  exists.
- The reset link works once, for 1 hour. Only the newest link works.
- Only a scrambled (hashed) version of the link's secret is stored.
- The reset page asks for the new password twice, with Show/Hide.
- After a reset, the account is logged out on every device.
- Email sending is NOT set up. While developing, the link is printed in
  the terminal running the app.

Password security:
- Passwords are stored only as scrypt hashes, never as plain text.


7. SELLER DASHBOARD (/seller/dashboard)
---------------------------------------
- Heading "Seller dashboard", "Welcome, <first name>. Manage <business
  name>'s listings here." and a "+ New listing" button.
- Four tiles: Total listings, Available (every listing is live, so this
  equals the total), Reserved and Sold. Reserved and Sold show
  "Coming soon", because they are not tracked yet.
- My listings: total kg available and how many listings have photos, then
  one row per listing, newest first, with:
    96px photo (or "No photo yet")
    name (links to the details page), a "Live" tag, and "No photos yet"
      if it has none
    quantity, location and price per kg in Naira
    first two lines of the description
    date listed, number of photos, and whether there is a video
    Edit, View and Delete buttons (Delete opens an "Are you sure?" box)
- Quick actions: Add a new listing, View marketplace, Manage listings.
- Your account panel: name, business name, account type, email, phone and
  business description.
- New listing form at the bottom (see section 9). "+ New listing" scrolls
  to it and puts the cursor in the first field.
- After a listing is added, the page reopens at the top with a message and
  the updated numbers.


8. BUYER DASHBOARD (/buyer/dashboard)
-------------------------------------
- Heading "Buyer dashboard", "Welcome, <first name>." and a "Browse the
  marketplace" button.
- Find raw materials: shortcuts to searches for cassava peels, rice husks
  and crop stalks.
- "Orders and saved searches" card, marked "Coming soon".
- Your account panel: name, company name (if given), account type, email
  and phone.


9. LISTINGS (SELLERS)
---------------------
Listing fields:
- Waste name or type (required, up to 100 characters)
- Description (required, up to 1000 characters; line breaks are kept)
- Quantity in kg (required, above 0, up to 2 decimal places)
- Price per kg (required, above 0, up to 2 decimal places)
- Location (required, up to 100 characters, free text)
- Contact phone number (required; starts as the seller's own number and
  can be changed for each listing)
- Photos and video (optional, see section 10)

Rules:
- Only sellers can add listings. A listing always belongs to the seller
  who is logged in.
- Only a listing's own seller can edit or delete it. Buyers and other
  sellers see "Not allowed", even if they type the address directly.
- Numbers like -5, "nan" or 1e5 are refused.
- Edits show on the marketplace straight away.
- Deleting asks "Are you sure?" in a pop-up box (Cancel or Delete), then
  removes the listing and its photo and video files. It cannot be undone.
- The date each listing was created is saved automatically.


10. PHOTO AND VIDEO UPLOADS
---------------------------
Limits:
- Up to 6 photos per listing: JPG, JPEG, PNG or WEBP, up to 5 MB each.
- Up to 1 video per listing: MP4 or WEBM, up to 50 MB.
- Very large uploads are refused as a whole, with a friendly message.

While choosing files (in the browser):
- A preview of each photo and the video appears straight away.
- Each chosen file has a Remove button, so it can be dropped before
  submitting.
- Choosing more photos adds to the earlier ones.
- Wrong types and files that are too big are refused with a message
  straight away.
- Without JavaScript, the plain file boxes still work.

On the server:
- Every file is checked again: its extension, its actual contents (so a
  renamed program or web page is refused), its size and the count.
- Each file is saved under a new random name (for example 3f9a...c2.jpg),
  never the uploader's own filename.
- Photos go in static/uploads/images/, videos in static/uploads/videos/.
- Uploaded files are served with headers that stop them running as code.

Editing:
- Current photos and the video are shown with "Remove" ticks. Ticked items
  show "Will be removed" before saving.
- More photos can be added, up to 6 in total.
- Choosing a new video replaces the old one.
- Removed and replaced files are deleted from disk after saving.

Missing files:
- If a file has been deleted from disk, pages still load. Missing photos
  are skipped or show a placeholder, and the next save tidies up the record.

Where media appears:
- Marketplace cards: cover photo, plus "x photos" and "Video" pills.
- Details page: photos and the video one at a time in a dark frame, with
  thumbnails and "Previous / 2 of 5 / Next" buttons.
- Seller dashboard: a photo for each listing.


11. MARKETPLACE (/marketplace)
------------------------------
- For logged-in users only (buyers and sellers), because listings show
  sellers' phone numbers.
- Heading "Agricultural waste marketplace".
- Filters in one row:
    Search: partial match on the waste type, capital letters ignored
    Category: shown, marked "Soon" (not built yet)
    Location: dropdown of every location used in listings
    Min price (₦) and Max price (₦)
    Sort by: shown, marked "Soon" (not built yet)
    Search button
  plus an "Available listings only" box marked "Coming soon", and a note
  that prices are per kg.
- "<n> listings match your search", with a "Clear all filters" link.
- Listing cards, newest first: photo (or "No photo yet"), an "Available"
  badge, photo and video pills, name, quantity and location, the seller's
  business name (or full name), and the price per kg in Naira. Clicking
  anywhere on a card opens it.
- "Previous / Page 1 of 1 / Next" (every listing is on one page for now).
- "No listings found" when nothing matches.
- Invalid prices in the filters show an error instead of breaking.

Listing details (/listings/<id>):
- Breadcrumb: Marketplace > listing name.
- Photo and video gallery (or "No photos yet").
- Available badge, name, location and price per kg.
- Facts: quantity available, value of the full lot (quantity x price),
  location and date listed. "Available from" and "Moisture" are shown as
  "Coming soon".
- Ordering panel marked "Coming soon" (online ordering with Paystack), with
  a switched-off "Order this lot" button and a "How buying will work" link.
- "Call the seller" button (tap to call) and the contact phone number.
- Edit and Delete buttons, shown only to the listing's own seller.
- Description panel.
- About the seller panel: business name, name, date they joined, business
  description, and "More from this seller" marked "Coming soon".


12. OTHER PAGES
---------------
- Contact form (homepage): name, email and message (up to 2000
  characters), all checked on the server. Messages are saved in the
  database (contact_messages table). There is no page for reading them,
  and no email notification.
- Terms and Conditions (/terms): a placeholder draft, clearly marked, that
  must be replaced with lawyer-reviewed terms before launch. The sign-up
  forms link to it.
- Error pages: friendly "Not allowed" (403), "Page not found" (404) and
  "Files too large" (413) pages.
- Messages (for example "Your listing has been added") appear as
  pop-up messages in the bottom-right corner: green for success, red for
  errors, each with an x to close it. Success messages close by themselves
  after 8 seconds.


13. DESIGN, RESPONSIVENESS AND ACCESSIBILITY
--------------------------------------------
Visual design:
- Colours: leaf green #1d3a2a, ink #16221a, paper #f4f6ef, mist #e3e9da,
  line #c9d3be, ochre #d49a1f (main buttons), white, muted #56645a,
  danger #a3221b and success #24613f. Light theme only.
- Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque headings, Public Sans text (16px).
- Rounded corners: 10px on cards and panels, 6px on buttons and fields.
- Buttons: ochre (main), white with a green border (secondary), dark green,
  and red-bordered (danger).
- Sticky dark-green header on public pages (transparent over the landing
  page photo), a sidebar on logged-in pages, and a dark ink footer.
- A farmland photo is fixed behind every page at 7% strength and partly
  grey, like a watermark. Phones get a portrait crop of it.
- Prices are in Naira, like ₦15,000 (kobo shown only when there are any).
- Real photographs of agriculture throughout, stored in the project.
  Credits for every photo are in the footer ("Photo credits").
- If a photo fails to load, a labelled panel is shown instead of a broken
  image.

Two navigation styles:
- Public pages (landing, log in, sign up, password pages, terms): a
  horizontal top bar with Home, About, Features, Contact, Login and Sign Up.
  Features goes to the "How it works" section.
- Logged-in pages (dashboards, marketplace, listing details, edit listing):
  a left-hand sidebar with the user's name and account type, then
    Overview (the dashboard), Marketplace,
    My listings (sellers only), My orders ("Soon", not a link yet),
    Profile (the account panel on the dashboard),
    the light/dark toggle and Log out.
  The current page has a tinted background and a green bar beside it.
  Notifications and Earnings are not shown, because they are not built.
- Error pages use whichever layout matches whether you are logged in.

Light and dark mode:
- A sun/moon toggle in the top bar (public pages) and the sidebar.
- Light: cream background, white cards, forest-green buttons, dark text.
- Dark: deep charcoal background, dark grey panels, light text, lighter
  greens. All text colour pairs meet the 4.5:1 contrast guideline.
- The choice is saved in the browser (localStorage) and remembered on every
  page and visit. With no saved choice, it follows the device setting.
- The saved theme is applied before the page is drawn, so there is no flash
  of the wrong theme.

Responsiveness (works from 320px phones to wide desktops, with no
sideways scrolling):
- Below 820px the public navigation becomes a hamburger menu that opens
  into a full-width green panel. It closes on Escape, on a tap outside it,
  or when a link is tapped.
- Below 960px the sidebar hides behind a menu button in a green top bar,
  and slides in from the left over a dark backdrop. It closes on Escape,
  the x button, a tap on the backdrop, or when a link is tapped.
- Grids reflow from one or two columns on phones to three or four on
  larger screens.
- The login and sign-up pages show a tall photo card beside the form, only
  on screens wider than 860px.

Accessibility:
- Every image has alt text, or is marked decorative.
- Form errors are linked to their fields, so screen readers read them out.
- A 3px ochre focus outline is visible for keyboard users, and a "Skip to
  content" link appears on the first Tab press.
- The "reduce motion" setting is respected.
- Text contrast was measured against the background photo.


14. SECURITY AND DATA
---------------------
Security:
- Protection against forged forms (a CSRF token) on every form.
- Login cookies cannot be read by scripts (HttpOnly) and are not sent with
  forms posted from other websites (SameSite=Lax).
- Dashboards are not stored by the browser, so the Back button cannot show
  them after logout.
- All database queries are parameterised (protected against SQL injection).
- Everything users type is escaped when shown, so typed HTML cannot run.
- Logout is POST-only.
- The session signing key is generated once and saved in
  instance/secret_key.txt (kept out of git), or taken from the SECRET_KEY
  environment variable.

Database tables (SQLite):
- users: name, email, phone, password hash, account type, business name,
  business description, terms-accepted date, created date.
- listings: seller, waste type, description, quantity (kg), price per kg,
  location, contact phone, created date.
- listing_media: one row per photo or video (listing, type, random file
  name, size, order, date). Rows are deleted with their listing.
- password_resets: hashed reset codes with an expiry time.
- contact_messages: messages from the contact form.
- Existing data is never deleted when the app adds new tables or columns.

Git ignores: instance/, database/*.db, static/uploads/, venv/, __pycache__/.

-----------------
- Email sending (password reset links, contact form notifications).

- Listing status (sold, reserved, paused). Every listing is always live.
  Shown as "Coming soon" on the seller dashboard.
- Sales, orders or view statistics.
- Categories (waste type is free text). Shown as "Soon" in the filters.
- Units other than kg (no tonnes, bags or truckloads).
- "Available from" date and moisture level for listings ("Coming soon").
- Location is free text (no list of Nigerian states).
- Pagination: all marketplace listings show on one page.
- Sorting options in the marketplace ("Soon" in the filters).
- "More from this seller" listings ("Coming soon").
- An admin area (for example to read contact messages or remove listings).
- Editing a user's profile after sign-up.
- Removing location data (GPS) from uploaded phone photos.
- Support for iPhone HEIC photos and MOV videos.
- Limits on repeated login attempts.
- Production setup: HTTPS, secure cookies, debug mode off, a production
  web server.
- Final Terms and Conditions text.
